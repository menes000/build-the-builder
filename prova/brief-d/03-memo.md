Re: Q4 early arrears campaign — parameter changes effective from the first call of the campaign

This memo takes precedence over the Early Arrears Telephony Standard v6 and over the Collections Operations mail thread of 29 September to 6 October wherever they differ. Everything it does not mention stands as written there.

1. Holidays. The Standard's list is extended by one date. Because of the core-banking migration the bank is closed on Friday 16 October 2026. For every date rule in the campaign (promise dates, business-day counting, callbacks) 16 October 2026 is a bank holiday. The full list for the campaign is therefore: 16 October, 29 October, 9 November, 25 November, 28 December and 31 December 2026.

2. Final band (21 to 50 days late). ONE persuasion attempt, not two. First and second bands keep two.

3. Floor. 50 percent of the overdue amount, rounded up to the next whole pound, for every product type. This replaces the 40 percent in the Standard and in Priya's mail. minimum_payment and instalment_amount remain unused.

4. Identity, order of questions. Ask the last four digits of the phone number FIRST, then the date of birth. The address-file migration means the phone check is the one we trust most, so it goes first.

5. Identity, wrong answers. The limit is TWO wrong answers in total, not three. The second wrong answer ends the check, whichever question it falls on. The day_month_swapped retry remains free.

6. Callbacks. Never for today. A callback is booked on the next business day or the business day after that, between 10:30 and 18:30 inclusive. One further ask if the first slot is outside this; then no booking.

7. "Already paid". The sentence is now: "A payment can take up to three business days to reach the account; if it has not shown by then, please call us." Still no promise on that product.

8. After a recorded promise the sentence is: "We will send you a confirmation by text within the hour." The SMS sentence in the Standard is withdrawn.
